## Structure

He also flags the long end of the Treasury curve and mortgage rates climbing, which raises refinancing costs for companies.

## 1. How he buys a stock
- Never goes all in on one day. He builds over about 3 to 6 months (sometimes 2 to 3 years) and sizes by risk: 1% to 2% of the portfolio for high-risk names, 3% to 7% for lower-risk ones.
- Examples of sizing: RH about 2% max (high risk, high upside), Netflix 5% to 7% (low risk), American Express (low risk, wants more). AMD is about 31% of the portfolio only because it went up, not because he bought that much; his AMD lots date from Dec 2024 to Apr 2025.
- Palantir: once 5,555 shares, now 1,000. Meta: lots from 2018 and 2022 (frames show 1,000 shares at a $99.13 average cost).
- His "number one key": more income than expenses, so a crash is a buying chance. He buys small amounts every week (and $250 a week in the Patreon portfolio). Tough-love section: fix income versus expenses before joining his private group, and look for side income.

## 2. The "emergency": Celsius (CELH)
- Source post (Beverage Insights, 4 weeks to Sep 19, Nielsen): Celsius Holdings volume +9.8% with +6.6% price, the Celsius brand alone down 20%, Alani Nu volume +74% with a 4% price cut. He walks through the math: the company grows because Alani offsets the weak core brand (Celsius is about 68% of volume, Alani about 32%).
- Why the core brand is weak, in his view: Celsius exited weak SKUs and Bloom Energy (launched 2024, big at Target, reportedly $500M+ revenue) is the nearest competitor. He thinks Bloom is a fad with VC-driven growth, citing Prime (and his $1M+ loss on TTCF) as a brand with great sales that still imploded.
- Counter to "Celsius is the next Prime": Prime was driven by influencers and kids; Celsius has an adult repeat customer base.
- Monster analogy: Monster's revenue growth slid from 93% (2005) to 73%, 49%, 14% and 10% (2009), and "peaked" arguments were made, yet the stock is up about 2,600% since Jan 2010. Do not assume a brand is finished during a rough patch.
- Category data: Red Bull volume +3.4%, Monster (excluding Bang) volume -1.6% with price +6.2%, Bang volume -24%, energy segment volume +8.8%.
- Call: he expects Celsius brand volume to return to growth within one to two quarters and the stock to run before the quarterly report once monthly data turns. He has been buying since late 2024; frames show about 6,415 shares at a $26.24 average cost.
