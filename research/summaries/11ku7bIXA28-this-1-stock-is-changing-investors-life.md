## Reaction 1: CNBC, why AMD benefits from AI agents
Agent apps (Meta Muse, OpenAI Dots, OpenClaw) run on virtual machines driven mainly by CPUs, not GPUs. AMD has the largest hyperscaler CPU share (the clip mentions its sixth-generation Venice Epyc on 2nm), and Nvidia is pushing its Vera CPU. An analyst (Jordan Klein) says Meta is lining up CPU supply from several vendors. Jeremy says he explained this a year or two ago, trusts Lisa Su because she delivered on a three-generation roadmap, and recalls when analysts doubted AMD could compete.
