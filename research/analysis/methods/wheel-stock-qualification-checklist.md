# Wheel stock qualification checklist

_Generated 2026-11-23 from `_data/`. Do not edit by hand._

Checking a candidate stock against fixed criteria before selling cash-secured puts on it as the first leg of the wheel: long-term uptrend, positive and reasonable P/E, consistent earnings beats and strong fundamentals. Used to avoid being assigned a stock in a downtrend.

## Step by step

- **Does:** Open a long-term price chart and draw a line from about a year and a half ago to the current price to confirm an upward trend.
- **Why:** The wheel is neutral to bullish, so the stock must be trending up in case of assignment.

### 2. Check P/E ratio is positive

- **Does:** Read forward and trailing P/E from a slide, then confirm the P/E history on a chart site.
- **Why:** A positive P/E (ideally under 100) shows the company is profitable; above 100 requires enough cash to cover debts.

### 3. Confirm consecutive earnings beats

- **Does:** Check the earnings record for consecutive quarterly beats and no misses.
- **Why:** Two missed quarters typically cause selling and a downtrend, which hurts a wheel position.
