Chart Read Backtest

Does the "Technical read on a stock chart" method hold up against what price did next? Calibration half only: 18 videos published up to 2026-10-30. Follow-up is the next 5 and 10 trading days after each video.

Read this first The 38 cases come from one market episode (a sharp fall that began just after the 2026-10-09 close), so they are not independent. Thresholds are a draft and the holdout half has not been looked at. The levels were spoken aloud, and the transcript cannot show what was on his charts.

Key: terms used on this page

CaseOne claim from one video, for example "a dip to 490 is a buy zone". There are 38.
As-of closeThe last closing price on or before the video's publish date. Every indicator uses only data up to this point.
LevelA price he said aloud: a support or buy-zone level, or an upside target. Shown as a dashed black line on the chart.
TouchedWithin the follow-up window, price traded down to the level (for a target: up to it).
Held / brokeHeld: after touching, the stock never closed more than 1% below the level. Broke: it did.
5-day, 10-day5 or 10 trading days after the as-of close.
Usual for this stockThe stock's average move over the same number of days across the previous two years. It is the comparison for "what would have happened anyway".
Band positionWhere price sits inside the Bollinger Bands: 0% is the lower band, 100% is the upper band, below 0% is outside the lower band.
RSIA 0 to 100 momentum gauge. Below 35 is treated here as oversold, above 70 as overbought.
Claim typesSupport / buy zone: he named a price where he expects buyers or would sell puts. Expects bounce: price at the lower band, 200-day or low RSI, no level given. Upside target: a price he expects it to reach. Pullback to middle band: he expects a dip to the 20-day average.
