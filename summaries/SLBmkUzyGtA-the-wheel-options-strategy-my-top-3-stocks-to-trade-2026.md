Method rules he states:
- Maximum 10% of the portfolio in puts on any one stock, split into three tranches of about 3.3%, at different strikes and dates.
- Puts: 25 to 30 days to expiry (45 days max, 60 only rarely), 20 to 30 delta. Covered calls: about 30 delta, 15 to 25 days, strike above assignment price (at assignment price if the stock falls hard).
- Target annualised return 30 to 40%. He says he is typically assigned every two to four months.
